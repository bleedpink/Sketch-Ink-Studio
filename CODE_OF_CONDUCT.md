# Code of Conduct

We want Sketch & Ink Studio to be a welcoming, respectful community. Be considerate, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and community participation that violate these expectations. If you experience or witness abusive behavior, contact the project maintainer through the repository's public contact method.
